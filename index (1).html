Scene 1 · The Beginning

1948

NIHON DŌGA 日本動画

Founded in 1948 as Nihon Dōga ("Japan Animated Films") by Kenzō Masaoka and Zenjirō Yamamoto, with the goal of building a Japanese animation industry on the scale of the big American studios. Every frame was drawn by hand.

It became Toei Dōga only in 1956, when Toei Company acquired it. The name Toei Animation followed in 1998.

Scene 2 · 1956–1970s

BUILDING A LEGACY

From theatrical colour films to weekly television, and out across the world. Tap a card for the story.

📽 Theatre first. Toei Dōga set out to make colour feature films for the family audience.

📺 Then television. From 1963 weekly TV series made animation a daily habit.

🌍 Then abroad. Series such as Mazinger Z found large audiences in Europe and beyond.

Six franchises that carried Toei to living rooms around the world. The heroes shown are original stand-ins for each genre.
